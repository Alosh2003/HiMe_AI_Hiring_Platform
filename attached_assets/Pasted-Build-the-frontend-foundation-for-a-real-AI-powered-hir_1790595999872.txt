Build the frontend foundation for a real AI-powered hiring platform called "HiMe AI".

Important: this is the FRONTEND ONLY phase.

Do NOT build the backend.
Do NOT use Supabase.
Do NOT use Lovable Cloud database/auth as the application backend.
Do NOT hardcode fake authentication logic.
Do NOT create a second backend inside the frontend.

The real backend will be developed separately using Laravel + PostgreSQL, and the AI service will be developed separately using Python + FastAPI.

PROJECT GOAL

HiMe AI is an AI-powered hiring platform connecting job seekers with companies.

The platform should allow:

1. Job seekers to create profiles and upload CVs.
2. AI to analyze CVs and extract professional information.
3. Companies to publish job vacancies.
4. AI to match candidates with jobs.
5. Candidates to see AI match scores and explanations.
6. Companies to see ranked candidates according to AI match scores.
7. Candidates to apply for jobs and track applications.
